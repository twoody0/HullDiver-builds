# Changelog

Newest first. The zips in this repo are always the newest build.

## 2026-11-13
- Co-op over the internet with nothing to set up: the host's invite code (Esc menu) now works
  for friends anywhere, with no port forwarding and no VPN. A friend on a different version of
  the game is told so instead of failing to connect.

## 2026-11-12 (evening)
- Islanders' clothes and hair stay outside their bodies: sitting down, a skirt or dress lies over
  the lap and the thighs instead of the legs coming up through it, coat tails and skirts rest on
  the bench, log, thwart or lounger instead of sinking into it, aprons stay clear of the legs, long
  hair falls over the shoulders instead of into them, and Pete's beard rests on his chest. A
  hand hanging at someone's side rests against the leg instead of in it.
- Shirt necklines no longer run up the front of the throat in a point.
- The sunbather lies properly along the lounger, heels on the towel; readers on the lookout bench
  sit where their feet reach the ground; people walking keep their feet on the sand, slopes and
  boards through every step.

## 2026-11-12 (later)
- Your hands in first person are real hands now: the same one-piece glove the divers wear, five
  separate fingers wrapped round each tool's handle, the thumb closed over them, instead of a
  plate with five tubes stuck on. Every finger closes round the handle and rests on it, side by
  side without passing into each other, the thumb shut over them like a real fist; the pry bar is
  held by its red grip.

## 2026-11-12

First public build. Highlights of what's in the game:

- Eleven boats to clean, from a dinghy to a coastal freighter, plus a sunken schooner, a ghost ship
  and a tall ship; grime in layers (slime, weed, crust, rust), barnacles, mussel clumps and old
  colonies; the boat's name and hidden finds under the grime.
- The island harbour: the pier, the dive shack and shop, the reef to restore, islanders with their
  own days, the water taxi's day-trippers, Pete fishing off the pier, boats riding the swell.
- Latest fixes:
  - Sterns: the transom is painted, fouled below the waterline and cleanable like the rest of the
    hull, and the sea meets it properly (no gap of open sky behind the stern's corners).
  - Nothing passes through anything: everything on a boat's deck, the seabed's rocks and coral
    heads, the anchors and the props are solid; fish, turtles, rays and jellies keep out of the
    sand, the hulls and the rocks; people step on and off boats through a gate in the rail and over
    the gunwale, and the water taxi has a landing stage with steps up to the pier,
    through a gap in the pier's rail; and islanders walking the beach go round behind the foot of
    the pier instead of under it.
  - Fishing: a hooked fish is always reeled all the way up before it's landed.
